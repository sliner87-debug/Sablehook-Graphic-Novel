PART 7

Before the summoning could begin, the heavy oak doors of the hall were perfectly sealed.
Without warning, the massive, intricate magical summoning circle etched into the stone floor flared to life.
The heavy, oppressive heat of the portal opening caused the wax of the surrounding candles on the stone table to melt and pool instantly.
Stepping gracefully from the roaring flames was Asmodeus, the Prince of the Hells.
His massive, cloven demonic hoof stepped heavily out of the flames and onto the medieval stone floor, instantly scorching the stone black where he stood.
Asmodeus looked around the hall, a terrifyingly charming smile revealing rows of dangerously sharp, inhuman demonic teeth.
But Sable did not flinch.
Faint, extremely complex magical legal runes embossed on the heavy leather cover glowed slightly as her mithral-gauntleted hand held it.
The heavy, ancient ledger hit the massive stone table with a heavy thud, sliding precisely to a stop directly in front of the Prince of the Hells.
Asmodeus looked across the table at her, a smirk of genuine respect and serious interest crossing his terrifying face.
Sable met his gaze without breaking, staring coldly across the table.
Standing behind her was Sovereign Banki.
Null-Fang radiated a deeply menacing, hungry dark aura.
To solidify his standing, Banki raised his gauntleted hand.
Immediately after, a cold gold glowing magical thread of the Divine snapped into place tightly alongside the blue and violet threads, perfectly completing the foundational Tri-Weave hidden-join structure.
The three threads wrapped perfectly around his fingers.
Asmodeus’s smirk entirely vanished.
His terrifying, glowing red eyes narrowed in sudden realization, caution, and anger.
Banki escalated the demonstration to terrifying levels.
The dark ribbon wove seamlessly into the others, summoning a towering, physical Tri-Weave Sovereign Throne behind him.
The Sovereign seated himself confidently in the massive, glowing throne of interwoven threads.
With serious respect for the power presented to him, Asmodeus accepted the terms.
Bringing it to the table, the towering demonic prince signed the heavy leather-bound infernal instrument.
The black flame quill scorched the heavy parchment contract as Asmodeus rapidly signed his elaborate, infernal demonic signature.
Sovereign Banki moved next.
He actively signed his name next to Asmodeus's on the glowing infernal parchment.
The instant the quills lifted, the signed parchment flared, glowing with heavy, binding magical seals.
A heavy wax seal on the glowing parchment melted and set simultaneously, branded perfectly by a mixture of red infernal fire and void-black Sovereign magic.
Upon execution of the contract, Asmodeus delivered the mandatory signing consideration immediately.
The business concluded, Banki utilized his magic, glowing threads of the Tri-Weave and the void-black Sovereign Thread swirling around his raised hand as he formally dismissed the Prince of the Hells.
The roaring hellfire of the massive magical summoning circle suddenly and violently collapsed inwards, sucking all the heat out of the dark, medieval room.
Sovereign Banki and Sable gathered tightly around the stone table, the smoke clearing.
Sable's delicate, highly-detailed mithral-gauntleted hand carefully traced the complex, heavy brass gears on the side of the machine.
The Engine's core glowed intensely with a contained red light.
Sovereign Banki and Sable looked at each other over the humming Concordance Engine.
Banki lifts the heavy brass-and-iron Concordance Engine off the massive stone table.
Banki and Sable walk toward the heavily warded oak doors of the Breakfast Court Hall.
