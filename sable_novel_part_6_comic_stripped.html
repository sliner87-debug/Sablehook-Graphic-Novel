PART 6

Sovereign Banki finally stepped down from the obsidian throne.
Banki bypassed the grand corridors and descended into a heavily fortified, candlelit strategic chamber buried deep within the keep.
His top officers were already waiting.
Orris Penn stood rigid in the shadows, holding thick, leather-bound ledgers detailing the city's corrupt finances.
High above them, clinging to the dark, vaulted stone ceiling, lurked Kestrel.
Banki approached the table and ran his hand over the parchment.
With a nod from Sable, the Rank 1 Shadow operatives deployed.
Within minutes, they were out in the city.
In a dirty, dimly lit backroom across the city, a greedy merchant noble frantically counted stolen gold coins.
He was wrong.
Seconds later, the backroom was empty.
But the ShadowNet was not just for assassination; it was a promise.
Pimwick worked in peace because perched on a stone gargoyle directly above his stall was a hooded Shadow operative.
But the ShadowNet required precision.
The order was given.
Down in the labyrinthine streets, the operation unfolded in perfect layers.
In the East Mouth industrial district, the primary strike team moved.
Inside, panic erupted.
But Kestrel had already anticipated the exit.
Before the Corrector could even scream, Kestrel crashed down from the stormy sky like a meteor.
Pinned firmly to the cobblestones under massive mithril talons, the Corrector screamed into glowing violet eyes.
The raid was a total, flawless success.
Back in the strategy room, Sovereign Banki watched the final magical nodes of the ShadowNet light up on the parchment.
Outside, the dark, towering gothic spires and smoking chimneys of Sablehook loomed under the oppressive night sky.
