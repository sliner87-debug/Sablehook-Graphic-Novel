PART 5

Banki held court from his towering obsidian throne.
Pell Ashwick thought he could outmaneuver a god.
He had been warned.
The consequence for lying to the sovereign was absolute.
A precise, ruinous lash of supernatural Hellfire snapped across the gothic hall.
When the flames receded, the hall fell dead silent, save for Pell’s ruined, bubbling, ragged breathing as he lay twitching in a pool of his own ash.
The interrogation continued.
Kestrel descended.
In her true form—a massive, insectoid Cliff-Skitter beast armored in the organic hide and silver mithril of the Blackfeather Sovereign Panoply—she was a nightmare made flesh.
When Pell was finally hanging on the absolute jagged edge of death, completely ruined in body, mind, and dignity, Kestrel paused.
Anchored permanently above the hall by an invisible telekinetic grip was a terrifying, humming construct known as The Lesson.
It was a jagged Magic Crystal Lattice built intricately around a uniquely rare, flawless Twin-Gem Reliquary, thoroughly laced with the pulsing, three-toned energy of the Tri-Weave—arcane purple, divine gold, and psionic blue.
At the exact moment of Pell's final, rattling breath, the crystal flared.
The hum of The Lesson still vibrated in the teeth of every noble present.
They expected judgment, perhaps a lesser punishment.
The miraculous light faded, leaving the heavy gloom of the hall intact.
Banki accepted.
Finally, the invisible backbone of the city was summoned.
Banki did not dismiss them.
